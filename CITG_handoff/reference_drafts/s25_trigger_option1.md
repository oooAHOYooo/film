(action)
The mother is failing. The Red-Eyes pile onto her, and her golden light dims under the weight of them.

(action)
Dallas's eyes go to the field bag in the mud beside him.

The blaster. Makayla's soother, rebuilt. And loaded in its deck — Sierra's cassette. Her recorder.
Her tape. The last of her voice in the world, and the creature's song caught on it.

Firing it means broadcasting it into the storm. Spending it. All of it, out into the rain, never
his again.

His hand goes to the bag — and stops. His thumb rests on the taped toggle.

He can't.

DALLAS
(barely audible, to himself)
I can't—

(action)
A hand closes over the bag and pulls it away from him.

Dominic. Soaked, bleeding from a bramble cut over one eye, his sister pinned somewhere behind the
wall of red. He looks at the rig once — the taped switch, the VU meter, the deck. He doesn't
understand a single component of it.

He understands exactly what it costs.

DOMINIC
(to Dallas, steady)
She'd want to be loud.

(action)
Dominic braces the blaster against his chest, aims it at nothing and everything, and flips the
toggle.

The VU needle PINS.

Sierra's recording tears out of the deck and into the storm — the creature's call, caught by her
hands, processed through a hundred of Dallas's nights, thrown out over the marsh at full gain.

For one second it is just one more voice against the wind.

Then, far out in the dark, one of the spherical recorders hears it.

(action)
The recorder tears up off its stake.
