# Scene 20: The Cry

EXT. THE FIELD - CONTINUOUS (s20.1)

They move in a ragged line through the grass — Makayla's LED ahead, Dallas behind her with the
field bag, Dominic last, learning with every step how little he knows this ground.

Then the wind drops for half a second.

And the CRY comes.

(action)
It rolls in from the direction of the marsh — long, high, and wrong, a sound with an animal's
grief in it and a machine's sustain, carrying over the whole field at once. It is the sound from
under the floorboards, out of the box, off the leash. The grass itself seems to lean away from it.

All three of them stop dead.

DOMINIC
That's it. 

MAKAYLA
It's calling her.

(action)
A beat — and then, from three different directions out in the dark, something dry and vast
RUSTLES in answer. Not a voice. Leaves. Acres of dead leaves, moving against the wind.

(action)
Dallas drops to one knee in the mud. He swings the field bag around, flips the flap, and checks
the blaster fast and sure — deck seated, cassette home, toggle taped down safe. His thumb brushes
the deck once. Then the flap closes, the strap cinches tight across his chest, and the bag rides
high on his back where it cannot be lost.

DALLAS
(standing)
Go.

(action)
No one says anything else. Makayla breaks into a run and the men follow, flat-out through the
grass, rain in their eyes, the cry still hanging in the air ahead of them and the dry rustling
gathering somewhere behind.
