# Scene 20: The Secret is Out

<!-- GOING TO FILM ON DAY 11 - 6.27.26 -->
INT. DOMINIC'S BASEMENT - CONTINUOUS (s20.1)

Dominic descends the basement stairs. He gazes in a daze upon all of the military-grade oscillators, tangles of thick coaxial wire, and Sierra's folder spread open. The shoebox under the heat lamp is empty. The small basement hopper window is busted open, rain blowing in from the outside.

Dallas frantically taps his receiver. The needle flickers back and forth between frequencies. 

DALLAS
It's gone. 

(action)
From the direction of the marsh—a cry emerges that sounds like the creature. 

DALLAS
(quiet)
It went back.

(action)
Before Dallas can move, DOMINIC appears at the top of the basement stairs holding Sierra's folder of anatomical creature sketches. His face is pale. He looks at Dallas like he doesn't know him.

DOMINIC
Dallas... what is this? 

(action)
Dallas makes eye contact with Dominic. Outside, the hum increases and Dallas' eyes fall toward the window. Out at the tall grass swaying in the dark. The Red-Eyes are pulsing at the edge of the property.

DALLAS
We have to get out there and find it before the other things do. 

DOMINIC
What other things?

(action)
Dallas looks at Dominic with a serious expression.

DOMINIC
Dallas. 

DALLAS
Dominic, listen—

DOMINIC
I listen. 

(action)
Dallas grabs one of the spherical recorders off the desk — one of Makayla's scuffed field recorders, pulled from the marsh days ago — and presses it into Dominic's hands. The little device is still warm, faintly humming.

DALLAS
Keep this on you. Whatever's out there — it knows these. It'll know you're with it.

DOMINIC
Keep who at bay?

(action)
Dallas points to the window. Dominic looks. In the distance, a pair of red eyes looks back at him.

Suddenly, the lights in the house flicker and within a moment, the room plunges into pitch black. 

Dominic opens a utility drawer, grabs the flashlights, and hands one to Dallas.

INT. LIVING ROOM (Continous) 

(action)
Dominic looks defeated. Dallas takes the flashlight from him.

DALLAS
You can stay here.

DOMINIC
Not anymore.

EXT. DOMINIC'S BACKYARD - CONTINUOUS (s20.4)

Dallas, Makayla, and Dominic step outside into the backyard. The hum is nearly deafening now.

DOMINIC
Do you think this will work?

(action)
Makayla nods.

(action)
Makayla drops to her knees, ripping open her gear bag. She scrambles out a portable speaker, slamming it onto the porch railing, aiming it directly at the creeping darkness like a shotgun.

She rips the wire with her teeth and bridges the oscillator into the circuit.

(action)
Makayla's hands fly over the oscillator dial, chasing the creature's pitch.

(action)
Makayla keys the broadcast.

A sound like thick glass cracking under pressure pierces the air. A high-pitched, localized frequency beam hits the lawn.

(action)
The grass at the edge of the yard sways rhythmically, in a direction opposite of the wind.

Dallas, Makayla, and Dominic watch stunned as the hum dissipates.

(action)
Dominic looks back at the tree line.

(action)
Makayla's jaw is tight. She keeps her hands on the dial.

DOMINIC
I think I am going to sit down, not feeling so good.

(action)
Dominic sits down on the outside porch.

(action)
Makayla lifts the sparking portable speaker. She sweeps the invisible acoustic beam across the yard like a lighthouse beam. A small glint of a red eye is glimmering in the distance. A wall of Red-Eye figures violently flinches, the brush of their bodies rustling as they peel back into the reeds, leaving a temporary, circular safe zone in the pouring rain.

The orbs gather dead leaves and reeds to themselves, swelling and taking the shape of tall, ragged figures, scarecrows of brush and grass with two red eyes burning in the middle of each.

But there are too many. Hundreds of red eyes begin to dot the tree line. The sheer numbers are overwhelming the small portable speaker.

From out in the grass, the creature's cry rises again. Dominic comes up off the porch. He looks onward and sprints towards the darkness.
