# Scene 22: The Blind

<!-- GOING TO FILM ON : Day 13 = Wednesday -->

EXT. THE WOODS - NIGHT (s22.1)

Dallas leads them through the trees at a run, rain hammering down through the canopy. He knows this
path — he has walked it once, in golden light, a lifetime ago this week.

Ahead, through the trunks: a faint glow, low to the ground.

EXT. / INT. THE WOODED BLIND - CONTINUOUS (s22.2)

The wooden dome sits lashed together at the edge of the woods, half-swallowed by fallen branches. A
work light burns inside. The low opening flickers with movement.

Dominic stops dead at the sight of it.

DOMINIC
What is this?

(action)
Dallas doesn't answer. He ducks through the opening. Dominic follows —

— and stops again, inside, taking it in.

Maps spread across the floor. His mother's marked-up charts, decades old, under Makayla's newer
bearing lines. Trail camera feeds. Coiled cable. Labeled tapes. A whole life, built in secret, a
quarter mile from his kitchen.

Makayla is at the gear bench, moving fast. She doesn't look up. She seats the portable speaker into
her bag, cinches it, checks the tracker.

DOMINIC
(quiet)
How long has this been here?

MAKAYLA
(still moving)
Longer than you'd like.

(action)
She shoulders the bag and crosses to the opening — then stops in front of Dominic. She takes the
little scuffed field recorder from her pocket, clips it to his jacket, checks that its light is
blinking, and pats it once.

MAKAYLA
It stays on you.

DOMINIC
Makayla — wait. Talk to me. What is all this? What was Mom—

(action)
She turns the tracker so both of them can see the screen.

The gold dot, deep in the marsh now, closing on the center. Around it, the red blips — no longer
scattered. A ring. Tightening.

MAKAYLA
It's going to its mother. It's going to make it there second.

(action)
A beat. She looks at Dallas — at the field bag on his back.

MAKAYLA
Keep up.

(action)
And she's out through the opening and gone into the dark, the green LED on her chest rig the last
of her they see.

Dallas and Dominic look at each other. Dominic looks once more around the blind — the maps, the
handwriting, all of it — then ducks out after her.

EXT. MARSH - EDGE - CONTINUOUS (s22.3)

The two men clear the treeline at a run. Ahead, the wall of tall grass thrashes in the wind, ten
feet high and black.

The green LED blinks once, far ahead, and the grass swallows it.

They go in.
