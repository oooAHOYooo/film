# SCENE 17D: THE HANDOFF

<!-- GOING TO FILM ON Day 11 -->

INT. DALLAS ATTIC - LATE AFTERNOON (s17d.1)

Dallas is dressed for life group — cleaner than usual, jacket on. He stands at his workbench
looking at the creature in its box. The device hums softly beside it, VU needle steady. The creature's
glow is warm and even. It's calm.

(action)
Dallas packs his field bag. Inside: the blaster rig — Makayla's soother modified, Sierra's cassette
loaded into the deck, alligator clips coiled, the toggle switch taped into place. He checks it once,
closes the bag and leaves it by the attic stairs.

(action)
He lifts the creature's box carefully. He can't leave it here alone tonight — not with the storm
coming, not with the hum outside getting bolder. Where he goes, it goes.

EXT. DALLAS HOUSE - CONTINUOUS (s17d.2)

(action)
Makayla is already waiting on the porch steps — like she knew. Tracker in hand. She falls in beside
him without a word. They walk toward Dominic's house, the box held against Dallas's chest.

DALLAS
It stays calm as long as it can hear the soother. If the line goes jagged, lower the gain.

MAKAYLA
I know how it works. I built it.

INT. DOMINIC'S BASEMENT - EARLY EVENING (s17d.3)

Dominic clears a corner of his basement workbench, not happy about any of this. Dallas sets the
shoebox under a clamp-on heat lamp and lays out the receiver and the small oscillator beside it.
The creature's glow is low and even. Asleep.

DOMINIC
Life group starts at seven. Nobody goes down here. Nobody.

DALLAS
It'll sleep. The soother's running. I'll go grab the rest of my gear and be back before it wakes.

(action)
Makayla circles the room, checking it like a perimeter. She stops at the small hopper window at the
top of the wall. The latch is stripped — the pane hangs a half-inch open, wind whistling through.

MAKAYLA
This latch is broken.

DOMINIC
It's been broken since we moved in.

(action)
She pulls the window shut and jams a paint stirrer into the frame as a wedge. Tests it. It holds —
mostly. She looks at the creature, then at Dallas.

DALLAS
It's a window, Makayla. It can't reach that.

(action)
Makayla doesn't answer. She sets the tracker on the bench where she can find it fast, and follows
Dominic upstairs.

EXT. DOMINIC'S HOUSE - MINUTES LATER (s17d.4)

(action)
Dallas cuts back across the yards toward his own house — moving quick, head down against the wind.
Behind him, the first headlights swing into Dominic's driveway. Life group is arriving.
