# Scene 13: The Confrontation

<!-- FILMING ON 
DAY 5 = 6.14.26 -->

EXT. DALLAS BACKYARD — MORNING (s13.1)

Dominic pulls up and kills the engine. He gets out and bends over to check the tire pressure on the back wheel — unhurried, incidental. 

Asher gets out on the passenger side and walks directly toward the edge of Dallas's property. 

(action)

Asher reaches the back gate and spots a trailcam mounted in the reeds. It's his. He untethers it and pockets the SD card without looking back. Meanwhile Dominic straightens up and knocks on Dallas's door.

Dallas emerges.

Asher has moved to the edge of the tall grass. He stands there, still. His hand finds the beads in his pocket.

Cut to.

EXT. DALLAS DOORWAY
Dallas walks outside and Dominic bangs his hand on the door. 

Dallas answers. 

DOMINIC
Dallas - do you mind coming a bit earlier today to watch Asher? I have to run to the store for lifegroup tonight. 

(action)
Dallas and Asher look at each other for a beat. 

DALLAS
Yeah that's fine. 

DOMINIC
Good. 

(action)
Dominic looks back at Asher, who hasn't moved from the edge of the yard. His eyes are on the Dallas' upstairs attic window. 

DOMINIC
Alright Asher, don't cause Mr. Dallas any trouble. 

(action)
Asher doesn't move. He keeps his eyes on the window.

(action)
Dominic drives away. 

EXT. DALLAS's HOUSE -  DOORWAY (s13.2)
Asher stares at Dallas for a good while - and reads his eyes.

(action)
Asher turns and points at the dead, flattened patch of grass at the edge of 
the tall grass. 

(action)
Dallas looks where he is pointing. His eyes widen. 

DALLAS
I don't know. 

(action)
Asher points to the trailcam. 

DALLAS
It looks like a trailcam. 

(action)
A whimper is heard from the kitchen. 

(action)
Asher goes still. He turns toward the sound, unhurried. He walks past Dallas and into the kitchen. He takes out his portable oscillator and sees that it is spiking as he points it to the ceiling.

Asher and Dallas stand silent looking at each other for a beat too long.

Asher rushes out of the room, up the stares, towards the attic. 

INT. DALLAS ATTIC - MOMENTS LATER (s13.3)

Dallas and Asher hover around the table at the makeshift aquarium Dallas constructed.

Asher takes his headphones off and grabs the ruby red headphones on the kitchen table. He puts them on and listens to the recording. 

(action)
Asher pulls out his portable viewfinder and begins to inspect the creature further. 

DALLAS
It's been here since last night. 

(action)
The pair look closer at the creature - it seems to breath through its back. 
Asher touches the creature gently - which causes the screen Oscillator around 
it to spark.

DALLAS
We can't tell anyone about this.

(action)
Asher turns to Dallas and nods in agreement. 

EXT. DALLAS HOUSE - PICNIC BENCH (s13.4)
Dallas adjusts his position on the picnic bench and gently walks towards the 
edge of the tall grass. Asher watches him, silent.

DALLAS
I found it a couple of miles away. I am not sure exactly. I was out walking. 

(action)
Asher keeps watching. Dallas keeps talking, half to the kid, half to himself.

DALLAS
I was alone. I ventured off the trail. It looked like it was injured. 

(action)
A beat.

DALLAS
I thought I could fix it. 

(action)
Dallas grabs a heat lamp and begins to examine the creature. Asher in the 
distance stands off, afraid to look at the creature.
