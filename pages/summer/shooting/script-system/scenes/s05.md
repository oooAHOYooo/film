EXT. MARSH TRAIL - LATER (s05.1)
The sun is dipping. Dallas and Dominic walk back home - along a wooden boardwalk that cuts through the tall, golden-yellow reeds.

Dominic and Dallas walk quietly for a while. Dominic looks out to the tall grass.

DOMINIC
The marsh sounds different before storms... Do you really think we'll get hit with a storm?

DALLAS
I'm not sure what you are talking about.

DOMINIC
Ah - forget it...

DOMINIC
Did you have a nice time at the picnic?

DALLAS
Yeah

DOMINIC
A little overwhelming no?

DALLAS
Yeah.

DOMINIC
Salt of the earth. 

(action)
DOMINIC
It's good to have you Dallas. 

(action)
In the distance a strange but distinct hum fills the air. 
Similar to the one that Dallas heard perviously. Dominic raises an eyebrow.

(action)
Dominic pulls out his phone, tapping an app.

DOMINIC
Hey check this out - It listens to the sounds and tells you the species of bird. 

(action)
Dominic struggles to get the app to work and begins to pace a bit. The hum dies down - leaving both confused. 

DOMINIC
Yeah - I like the birds - I like the sounds they make - I thought about how to write them down. And then I never thought much past it. I'm 'writing now - but something else - I don't got much time. ' one day I'll tell a story of these marlins.

(action)
He takes a slow, deep breath, collecting himself.

DOMINIC
At least I hope I will. 

(action)
Dallas looks onward. 

DOMINIC
This whole existence - being alive - its so beautiful - so everlasting - so tragic. 

DALLAS
Yeah

DOMINIC
And then - boom - Gone. 

(action)
Dallas and Dominic walk slowly and Dallas looks out to the tall grass for a while.
