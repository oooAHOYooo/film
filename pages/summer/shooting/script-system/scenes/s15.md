# Scene 15: The Tracking Network

INT. DALLAS ATTIC
Dallas stands alone and looks at the creature. His phone rings. 

INT. DALLAS ATTIC

(action)
Makayla looks at Dallas and then at the creature and pulls out a small prototype from her backpack. She holds it out.

The two look at each other for a moment.

DALLAS
What is that?

MAKAYLA
A basic amplifier.

(action)
She turns to the creature and turns it on. The creature starts to wiggle.

Behind the creature's smooth sine wave, a second signal appears—jagged, arrhythmic. It pulses in sharp, irregular bursts, riding underneath the creature's frequency like static beneath music.

MAKAYLA
(quiet)
That's not coming from the creature. That's drawing from outside.

(action)
The two look at the creature and then at the oscilloscope and then at the sway of the tall grass outside.

MAKAYLA
Dallas. I have to show you something.

INT. DALLAS HOUSE – LATE AFTERNOON (s14c.1)

Late-day light leaks through the kitchen blinds. The creature is hidden beneath a towel inside the makeshift enclosure. A SOFT, IRREGULAR HUM bleeds faintly into the room.

Dallas stands rigid at the counter, tightening a cable connection.

A KNOCK at the door.

Dallas freezes.

Another knock—cheerful.

DOMINIC (O.S.)
Dallas? You home? I think you might have our step ladder.

INT. DALLAS HOUSE – FRONT ENTRY – CONTINUOUS (s14c.2)

Dallas opens the door. Dominic stands there, winded.

DOMINIC  
There he is! Hey—sorry to bug you. The storm knocked something loose on our gutter.

Dallas forces a smile.

DALLAS  
Weather’s been strange.

DOMINIC  
Tell me about it—Pat’s already texting doom.

They share a short laugh. The HUM beneath the house deepens—to almost imperceptible.

Dominic tilts his head.

DOMINIC  
You hear that?

DALLAS  
Hear what?

Dominic listens another beat. Shrugs.

DOMINIC  
Probably my sinuses. Allergies. Or ghosts.  

(then)  
Mind if I set the ladder by your fence?

DALLAS  
Sure.

---

INT. DALLAS KITCHEN – CONTINUOUS (s14c.3)

Dominic steps inside. The HUM vibrates a glass of water on the counter—just enough to ripple.

Dallas slides between Dominic and the enclosure casually.

DOMINIC  
Wow. Place looks like NASA threw up.

DALLAS  
Work stuff.

DOMINIC  
You educators—always overprepared.

Dominic steps once more toward the counter—toward the towel.

Dallas moves **immediately**.

DALLAS  
So—uh—weren’t you fixing the porch light too?

DOMINIC  
Yeah—

The creature STIRS beneath the towel. A muted WHIMPER—felt more than heard.

The oscillators flicker GOLD for half a second.

Dominic freezes.

DOMINIC  
Okay. That—

Dallas reaches out and SLAMS a power switch. Darkness. Silence.

A beat.

DOMINIC  
…Storm’s coming quick.

Dallas smiles too fast.

DALLAS  
We should all stay inside tonight.

Dominic nods slowly. Something unsettled—but unspoken.

DOMINIC  
Yeah.  
(then, lighter)  
Alright—ladder can wait. Dinner smells like it’s burning anyway.

He turns to leave.

At the door, Dominic pauses.

DOMINIC  
Dallas?

DALLAS  
Yeah?

DOMINIC  
If you ever need help—  
(gestures vaguely)  
—with whatever this is… just ask.

Dallas nods.

Dominic leaves.

Silence.

Dallas stares at the towel—at the faint GOLDEN GLOW bleeding through.

EXT. NEIGHBORHOODS - EVENING (s15.1)
Dallas and Makayla walk toward the neighborhood, through a long stretch of golden-hour woodland, until an open field spreads before them. At the far end, a row of tall trees. Dallas stops and looks around.

(action)
As they cut through the reeds, Makayla stops every so often to press small devices into the mud at the base of the stalks. Each one blinks a slow, faint amber as it powers on — new nodes, joining a net that has been out here far longer than Dallas has.

Dallas watches a scatter of them pulse in the dusk.

DALLAS
How many of these are out here?

MAKAYLA
Enough to cover the whole marsh. These are for your side. The signal moved when you did.

(action)
The two walk into the field and find a small wooden chest on the ground, hidden beneath tall grass.

EXT. MAKAYLA'S WOODED SPOT - CONTINUOUS (s15.2)
A small wooden structure, hidden by fallen trees and branches.

(action)
Inside, a wooden box. Makayla opens it. Trail cameras. Recording equipment. Cables and wires coiled at the bottom, connected to a small portable oscillator.

(action)
Makayla turns the oscillator on. It lights up.

(action)
Dallas crouches and studies the wiring. He looks at one of the devices on the ground.

DALLAS
Did you build all this?

MAKAYLA
Most of it. Some pieces were my mom's.

(action)
Makayla lifts a battered waterproof notebook from the box and opens it flat on the lid. A hand-drawn map of the marsh. The creek bends, the inlet, the far treeline — all rendered carefully. Bearing lines radiate from marked points along the perimeter, each labeled with a number. At the end of each: a small circle.

MAKAYLA
My mom drew the first version. I keep it current.

(action)
Dallas pulls his own small notebook from his jacket and finds the bearing sketch from that morning — two lines, an arrow, the X. He holds his sketch next to her map.

His bearing line, extended, points directly at position four.

(action)
Neither of them speaks. Makayla looks at his sketch, then at him.

(action)
Makayla sets up a pocket tripod and clips in a small microphone. She positions it toward the tree line and hits record.

DALLAS
What are you listening for?

MAKAYLA
I'm not listening for something specific. I just record. The signal separates from the noise if you do it long enough.

(action)
Makayla lifts a smaller wooden box from inside the main box and holds it out to Dallas. He opens it and finds a spare oscillator inside, wrapped in cloth. Dallas holds it.

MAKAYLA
That one's yours. Backup.

(action)
Makayla reaches back into the main box and pulls out a portable wooden device — battery-powered, compact. Two metal rods extend from it: contact transducers. She sets it on the ground.

MAKAYLA
This one's different. Hold the rods.

(action)
Dallas looks at them.

DALLAS
What does it do?

MAKAYLA
Just hold them.

(action)
Dallas grips the rods. A wild, overwhelming sensation hits him. In the distance a cry rises — faint and distinct. Dallas drops to his knees, head shaking.

(action)
Makayla pulls the rods from his hands. Dallas stays on his knees for a moment, getting his breath back.

(action)
In the distance the howls deepen. The two of them go still.

(action)
Dallas pulls out his pocket recorder and starts recording, still unsteady.

DALLAS
(low, after a long pause)
We should go.
