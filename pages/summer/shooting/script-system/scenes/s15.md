# Scene 15: The Tracking Network


<!-- s15 -->
<!-- FILMING ON DAY 4 -->
EXT. NEIGHBORHOODS - EVENING (s15.1)
Dallas and Makayla walk toward the neighborhood, through a long stretch of golden-hour woodland, until an open field spreads before them. At the far end, a row of tall trees. Dallas stops and looks around.

EXT. OUTER MARSH - EVENING (s15.15 )
(action)
As they cut through the reeds, Makayla stops every so often to press small devices into the mud at the base of the stalks. Each one blinks a slow, faint amber as it powers on — new nodes.

Dallas watches a scatter of them pulse in the dusk.

DALLAS
How many of them are out here?

MAKAYLA
How many of what?

DALLAS
The nodes. 

MAKAYLA
Enough to cover the whole marsh. 

DALLAS
Why?

MAKAYLA
Because there's something has been living out here. For a long, long time. And I'm going to prove it. 


(action)
The two walk into the field and towards a small wooden structure, hidden by fallen trees and branches.


EXT. MAKAYLA'S WOODED SPOT - CONTINUOUS (s15.2)

The two approach the wooden structure and enter.  

(action)
Makayla unveils a blanket - filled with different recording equipment. 
Makayla opens it.  Cables and wires coiled at the bottom, connected to a small portable oscillator.

(action)
Makayla opens up a portable computer monitor resting on a makeshift table of found objects. 


(action)
Makayla turns on the computer and begins to examine the marsh at large. A computer-drawn map of the marsh. The creek bends, the inlet, the far treeline — all rendered carefully. Bearing lines radiate from marked points along the perimeter, each labeled with a number. At the end of each: a small circle.

MAKAYLA
Fascinating. I need to download this.


(action)
Makayla sets up a pocket download device.
 She positions it toward the tree line and hits record.

DALLAS
What are you listening for?

(action)
Makayla turns off the device. 

MAKAYLA
I'm not sure. I just record. 

(action)
Makayla takes out the audio sd cards from the device and hands them to Dallas. 

MAKAYLA
For you. 

(action)
Makayla reaches back into the main box and pulls out a portable wooden device — battery-powered, compact. Two metal rods extend from it: contact transducers. She sets it on the ground.

MAKAYLA
It was supposed to be an amplifer - I'll finish it one day.

(action)
Dallas looks at the device - Dallas picks it up.

MAKAYLA
You could take it. 

(action) 
In the distance a cry rises — faint and distinct. 
Dallas touches his temple and eyes shakes a little.

(action)
Dallas gets his breath back. 

(action)
Dallas looks down at the device. 

(action)
In the distance the howls deepen. 

MAKAYLA
(low, after a long pause)
We should go.
