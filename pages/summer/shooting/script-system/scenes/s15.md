# Scene 15: The Tracking Network


<!-- s15 -->
<!-- FILMING ON DAY 4 -->
EXT. NEIGHBORHOODS - EVENING (s15.1)
Dallas and Makayla walk toward the neighborhood, through a long stretch of golden-hour woodland, until an open field spreads before them. At the far end, a row of tall trees. Dallas stops and looks around.

(action)
As they cut through the reeds, Makayla stops every so often to press small devices into the mud at the base of the stalks. Each one blinks a slow, faint amber as it powers on — new nodes.

Dallas watches a scatter of them pulse in the dusk.

DALLAS
How many of these are out here?

MAKAYLA
Enough to cover the whole marsh. These are for your side. The signal moved when you did.

(action)
The two walk into the field and find a small wooden chest on the ground, hidden beneath tall grass.

EXT. MAKAYLA'S WOODED SPOT - CONTINUOUS (s15.2)
A small wooden structure, hidden by fallen trees and branches.

(action)
Inside, a wooden box. Makayla opens it. Trail cameras. Recording equipment. Cables and wires coiled at the bottom, connected to a small portable oscillator.

(action)
Makayla turns the oscillator blaster gun thing on. It lights up.

(action)
Dallas crouches and studies the wiring. He looks at one of the devices on the ground.

DALLAS
Did you build all this?

MAKAYLA
Most of it. Some pieces were my mom's. Some were my uncles. Fairytales. 

(action)
Makayla lifts a battered waterproof notebook from the box and opens it flat on the lid. A hand-drawn map of the marsh. The creek bends, the inlet, the far treeline — all rendered carefully. Bearing lines radiate from marked points along the perimeter, each labeled with a number. At the end of each: a small circle.

MAKAYLA
I have been all around Branford -  leaving markings. For years. And nothing. Nothing until the other night.  

(action)
Makayla sets up a pocket tripod and clips in a small microphone. She positions it toward the tree line and hits record.

DALLAS
What are you listening for?

MAKAYLA
I'm not sure. I just record. The signal separates from the noise if you do it long enough.

(action)
Makayla lifts a smaller wooden box from inside the main box and holds it out to Dallas. He opens it and finds a spare oscillator inside, wrapped in cloth. Dallas holds it.

MAKAYLA
For you. 

(action)
Makayla reaches back into the main box and pulls out a portable wooden device — battery-powered, compact. Two metal rods extend from it: contact transducers. She sets it on the ground.

MAKAYLA
It broadcasts. 

(action)
Dallas looks at the device..

(action)
Dallas grips the rods. A wild, overwhelming sensation hits him. In the distance a cry rises — faint and distinct. Dallas drops to his knees, head shaking.

(action)
Makayla pulls the rods from his hands. Dallas stays on his knees for a moment, getting his breath back.

(action)
In the distance the howls deepen. The two of them go still.

(action)
Dallas pulls out his pocket recorder and starts recording, still unsteady.

MAKAYLA
(low, after a long pause)
We should go.
