# Scene 21: Into the Marsh

EXT. THE FIELD - DEEPER - CONTINUOUS (s21.1)

The run.

Makayla's green LED bobs ahead through the stalks — there, gone, there again. She never checks the
tracker at a full stop anymore; she reads it on the move, cutting angles through the grass like
she has run this exact line a hundred times. She has.

Dallas holds the middle, the field bag tight on his back, one hand coming off his strap again and
again to steer Dominic — around a sinkhole, over a downed fence post, left where the ground goes
soft.

Dominic runs on faith. Every landmark he owns is behind him. The grass is over his head now, the
ground wetter with every stride — lawn, then field, then something that pulls at his shoes and
doesn't want to give them back.

DOMINIC
(gasping, to Dallas)
How does she know where she's going?

DALLAS
Same way I do. Your mother drew the map. Your sister memorized it.

EXT. THE FIELD - MARSH EDGE - CONTINUOUS (s21.2)

The grass changes. Softer stalks, then reeds. The mud takes Dominic to the ankle and he goes down
hard on one knee — and Dallas's hand is already there, hauling him up without breaking stride.

DALLAS
Feet wide. Weight forward. You're okay.

(action)
Dominic nods, soaked to the skin, mud to the shins now, and keeps moving. Somewhere in the last
hundred yards he has stopped being led and started following — it looks the same from outside,
but it isn't.

Ahead, the field ends. A black line of trees rises against the storm — the woods that stand
between the neighborhood and the true marsh. Makayla's LED slips into the trunks and vanishes.

(action)
Dallas pulls up at the treeline for one breath, getting his bearings — and finds them. He knows
this path. He has walked it once, in golden light.

DALLAS
Blind's through here. Stay on me.

(action)
He hitches the field bag once — the blaster's weight settling against his back — and plunges into
the trees. Dominic goes in behind him, and the field closes up as if no one had ever crossed it.
