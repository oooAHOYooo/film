# Scene 19: The Whole Truth

EXT. THE FIELD - TALL GRASS - NIGHT (s19.1)

Dallas and Dominic crash through the first wall of grass, rain driving sideways, stalks whipping
their faces. The street noise is already gone. There is only wind.

Ahead, in a low break where the grass thins to knee height, a green LED holds still.

Makayla. Standing in the open, tracker up, waiting. She was never running from them. She was
waiting for them to catch up.

DOMINIC
(breathing hard, closing the distance)
Makayla. Stop. Whatever this is, it stops right now. We go home, we—

MAKAYLA
(not looking up from the tracker)
It can't stop. Look.

(action)
She turns the tracker to him. The gold dot, deep in the black of the screen, moving in a clean
straight line. The red blips around it, sliding inward from every edge.

MAKAYLA
That's the little one Dallas has been keeping alive in your basement. It's hurt, and it's going to
the center of the marsh, because that's where its mother is. And those—

(action)
Her finger touches the red.

MAKAYLA
—are going to get there first. Unless we don't let them.

DOMINIC
(rain running off his face)
How do you know all this? How do you know any of this?

MAKAYLA
Because I've been listening to it since before I knew what I was listening to. Mom built the
network. The recorders, the bearings, the blind. She never finished it. I kept it running.

(action)
A beat. She looks at Dallas — at the field bag on his back.

MAKAYLA
He finished it.

(action)
Dominic turns. Dallas pulls the bag around on its strap and opens the flap just enough. Inside,
padded in foam: the blaster — the rebuilt soother, dials taped, the cassette deck seated in its
cradle. Rain beads on the housing. Dallas's hand rests on it a moment, almost gentle.

DALLAS
Your mother started building a way to answer it. Mine caught its song on tape. This is both of
those, working. I tested it three nights ago. It works.

DOMINIC
(quiet)
And it does what.

DALLAS
It gives the little one its own voice back — loud enough to matter. Loud enough to clear a path
through those things.

(action)
Dominic looks at the tracker. The red ring, tightening. The gold dot, alone inside it. Somewhere
under everything, faint through the storm, the hum.

He has spent the whole night lying to forty people. He is done lying to himself.

DOMINIC
Then why are we standing here?

(action)
Makayla almost smiles. She kills the tracker's backlight, turns, and moves — into the deep grass,
the green LED on her chest rig the only light in the field.

Dallas cinches the bag shut and shoulders it. The two men go after her.
