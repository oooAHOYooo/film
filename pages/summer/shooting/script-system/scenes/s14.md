# Scene 14: The Connection

<!-- FILMING ON = DAY 4 = 6.13.26 -->
EXT. DALLAS BACKYARD - OLD PORCH TABLE (s14.1)

Makayla and Dallas sit outside the patio. Neither of them says anything for a while.

Asher sits alone a few feet away—he begins to tap on his fingers, and then nods in approval. 


MAKAYLA
What do you do when you are sad?

(action)
Dallas looks at the ground and then at Makayla.

DALLAS
I don't know.

MAKAYLA
Your wife was an epistemologist?

(action)
Dallas looks at the sky and then at the ground and then at the dead patch of grass at the edge of the property.

MAKAYLA
She studied how fish communicate underwater?

DALLAS
She did.

MAKAYLA
Sounds like a smart lady.

DALLAS
(long beat)
She was. How did you know that?

MAKAYLA
I've read her papers. Her book. Her talks. And wondered what kind of woman she was. And now — here I am — with her husband.

DALLAS
Yeah.

MAKAYLA
You're not really like who I thought you would be.

(action)
Dallas nods and looks at the ground.

MAKAYLA
Our mom's gone too. Me and Asher — and our uncle. That's the house now. But we're still here. Branford.

(action)
Dallas looks at the ground.

(action)
A gentle breeze blows through the sky. Asher begins to pace around and bob his head a bit back and forth. Suddenly, he stops in his tracks and proceeds to walk slowly into the house. 

Makayla looks back at him and continues to fiddle with the Oscillator.

(action)
Dallas sits for a while on the long old picnic table outside his house. In the distance the creature begins to cry and then it fades. Dallas looks down at the trailcam in the distance. 

(action)
He looks at the recorder in his hand. He has been recording its sound all along.

(action)
The breeze overwhelms him and in the distance he begins to hear the faint pulse of a dark deep hum. 

MAKAYLA
Dallas, we can't tell anyone about this. 

(action)
A loud thump is heard from inside. Makayla runs into Dallas's kitchen.

INT. DALLAS' KITCHEN - MOMENTS LATER (s14.4)

Makayla holds Asher by the head as he shakes on the ground. She begins to sway and hum to him gently. She puts her hand on his chest and Asher breathes.

DALLAS
Is he okay?

MAKAYLA
He's fine. He's just doing Asher stuff. I think he's processing.

(action)
Makayla takes a deep breath as Asher shakes violently and stops shaking violently.

MAKAYLA
Sit up.

(action)
Makayla makes Asher sit up and brings him a glass of water. As she is pouring it — her eyes find the creature sleeping peacefully below the heat lamp.

She sets the glass down.

MAKAYLA
(quiet)
Dallas.

(action)
Dallas looks around at his kitchen island — which has turned part into a triage hospital station. A portable oscilloscope is wired up to the creature. The device pulses in a rhythmic, golden jagged line — in sync with the creature's heartbeat.

In the distance they hear howls — and then silence again.

The creature begins to fade. Dallas turns the dial into another frequency range and waits until it is asleep. Makayla watches the dial, then watches Dallas.

MAKAYLA
I find it interesting that your wife was an epistemologist. They used to think the sea was silent — but it's not.

DALLAS
(whispering)
It changes the frequency I am broadcasting. I'm trying to get a response.

(action)
The two look at the creature on the table. The creature seems to be responding to it.

(action)
Makayla looks at Dallas and then at the creature and pulls out a small prototype from her backpack. She holds it out.

The two look at each other for a moment.

DALLAS
What is that?

MAKAYLA
An amplifier.

(action)
She turns to the creature and turns it on. The creature starts to wiggle.

Behind the creature's smooth sine wave, a second signal appears—jagged, arrhythmic. It pulses in sharp, irregular bursts, riding underneath the creature's frequency like static beneath music.

MAKAYLA
(quiet)
That's not coming from the creature. That's drawing from outside.

(action)
The two look at the creature and then at the oscilloscope and then at the sway of the tall grass outside.

MAKAYLA
Dallas. I have to show you something.

SCENE 14B: THE FIELD JOURNAL


s14B.1 — EXT. DALLAS BACKYARD — OLD PICNIC TABLE — CONTINUOUS
Dallas and Asher sit in silence. Asher, a few feet off, threads beads onto a long string — one bead for every listening post he has staked out in the marsh. The quantity matters; we just don't know why yet.

Dallas reaches into his bag and pulls out a soft-covered field journal. Sierra's. The cover is warped from water, held together with a rubber band. The one from her folders he keeps pushing closed. He doesn't open it right away. He holds it in his lap.

DALLAS
(quiet)
Hers. 

(action)    
He sets it on the picnic table. Asher looks at it but doesn't touch it.

(action)
Dallas opens it. The first pages are careful — a woman's handwriting, pencil sketches of the marsh, dated entries. 
Observations. Frequencies. 

(action)
A small drawing of a Golden creature in the margin — recognizable. Similar to the one in his kitchen.

(action)
Dallas turns the page. The sketches get more detailed. Different Golden creatures at different sizes.

(action)
Dallas turns another page and stops. On the left side of the spread: a Golden creature, curled and peaceful. On the right side: the same body, the same proportions, the same ears, the same frame, gone wrong. The fur is matted and colorless like dead grass. There are eyes where there were no eyes before, and they are red.

(action)
An arrow between them. No words.

DALLAS
(quiet)
What am I looking at?

(action)
Asher has come around the table. He looks at the spread. He doesn't look surprised.

(action)
Dallas stares at the page. His thumb traces the arrow.

DALLAS  
They're the same.

(action)
Asher nods.

(action)
A beat.

DALLAS
One turns into the other?

(action)
Asher stops threading. He puts one finger on the peaceful creature. Then he drags it slowly across the arrow to the red-eyed one. Then he points at the kitchen window. Toward the creature inside.

DALLAS
When they get hurt. They change.

(action)
Asher goes still. That is the answer.

DALLAS
The one I found was injured.

(action)
A gust of wind moves through the tall grass at the edge of the property. Asher's head turns toward it. Just slightly.

(action)
Dallas closes the journal carefully.

DALLAS
She saw one. 

(action)
The wind picks up again. In the far distance, barely audible — not quite a howl, not quite a hum. Something in between.

(action)
Asher stops threading.

ASHER
(quietly, to himself)
They're listening.

(action)
Dallas turns to him. He doesn't elaborate. He goes back to his beads.

(action)
Dallas picks up the journal and slides it back into his bag.

DALLAS
We should go inside. Check on it.

(action)
They stand. Dallas glances back at the tall grass one more time before going in.

INT. DALLAS KITCHEN — AFTERNOON (s14b.2)

Asher is at the kitchen table, head down, pencil moving. Not doodling — working. The drawing has been going for a while.

Dallas comes in from the back door. He sets Sierra's recorder on the counter and stops.

He comes around behind Asher and looks at the paper.

It's the marsh. The creek bends, the inlet, the far treeline — all rendered carefully. Bearing lines radiate from three separate points along the perimeter. Each labeled with a number. At the end of each: a small circle.

(action)
Dallas goes to the counter drawer. He pulls out his small notebook and finds the bearing sketch from that morning — two lines, an arrow, the X.

He holds his sketch next to Asher's map.

His bearing line, extended, points directly at position four.

(action)
Neither of them speaks.

(action)
Asher sets down his pencil. He turns and points through the kitchen window — toward his house, across the street.

(action)
Dallas looks at the house. Looks back at the map.

He understands.

INT. DALLAS HOUSE – LATE AFTERNOON (s14c.1)

Late-day light leaks through the kitchen blinds. The creature is hidden beneath a towel inside the makeshift enclosure. A SOFT, IRREGULAR HUM bleeds faintly into the room.

Dallas stands rigid at the counter, tightening a cable connection.

A KNOCK at the door.

Dallas freezes.

Another knock—cheerful.

DOMINIC (O.S.)
Dallas? You home? I think you might have our step ladder.

INT. DALLAS HOUSE – FRONT ENTRY – CONTINUOUS (s14c.2)

Dallas opens the door. Dominic stands there, winded.

DOMINIC  
There he is! Hey—sorry to bug you. The storm knocked something loose on our gutter.

Dallas forces a smile.

DALLAS  
Weather’s been strange.

DOMINIC  
Tell me about it—Pat’s already texting doom.

They share a short laugh. The HUM beneath the house deepens—to almost imperceptible.

Dominic tilts his head.

DOMINIC  
You hear that?

DALLAS  
Hear what?

Dominic listens another beat. Shrugs.

DOMINIC  
Probably my sinuses. Allergies. Or ghosts.  

(then)  
Mind if I set the ladder by your fence?

DALLAS  
Sure.

---

INT. DALLAS KITCHEN – CONTINUOUS (s14c.3)

Dominic steps inside. The HUM vibrates a glass of water on the counter—just enough to ripple.

Dallas slides between Dominic and the enclosure casually.

DOMINIC  
Wow. Place looks like NASA threw up.

DALLAS  
Work stuff.

DOMINIC  
You educators—always overprepared.

Dominic steps once more toward the counter—toward the towel.

Dallas moves **immediately**.

DALLAS  
So—uh—weren’t you fixing the porch light too?

DOMINIC  
Yeah—

The creature STIRS beneath the towel. A muted WHIMPER—felt more than heard.

The oscillators flicker GOLD for half a second.

Dominic freezes.

DOMINIC  
Okay. That—

Dallas reaches out and SLAMS a power switch. Darkness. Silence.

A beat.

DOMINIC  
…Storm’s coming quick.

Dallas smiles too fast.

DALLAS  
We should all stay inside tonight.

Dominic nods slowly. Something unsettled—but unspoken.

DOMINIC  
Yeah.  
(then, lighter)  
Alright—ladder can wait. Dinner smells like it’s burning anyway.

He turns to leave.

At the door, Dominic pauses.

DOMINIC  
Dallas?

DALLAS  
Yeah?

DOMINIC  
If you ever need help—  
(gestures vaguely)  
—with whatever this is… just ask.

Dallas nods.

Dominic leaves.

Silence.

Dallas stares at the towel—at the faint GOLDEN GLOW bleeding through.
