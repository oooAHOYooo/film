INT. JACE'S BASEMENT (s07.1)
Dallas looks around in amazement. The basement is covered with normal basement things - but as his eyes scan the room he notices that there are two beds, room, and clutter. Around the corner there is a workstation filled with old CRT machines and what not.

(action)
Dallas looks at all of the different audio and acoustic devices scattered across the room. On the cieling there are tiny beeds hung from strings - tiny with different colors. 

DOMINIC
This was formerly our shared basement - but, now, my brother has occupied it. He just loves this basement... Here, help me grab the fan. 

(action)
Dallas looks at all of the different audio and acoustic devices scattered across the room. Dallas walks over to one and sees the similar trail cam that he found on his property - but, this one is different. It has a small antenna attached to it. 

Dominic taps Dallas on the shoulder, breaking him out of his gaze and together they slowly move the AC unit upstairs. 

INT. DOMINIC'S BASEMENT (s07.2)

(action)
Dominic helps Dallas shove a heavy oak table against the wall. As they move - Dominic knocks over a photo frame - and Dallas grabs it. On the ground is a small, framed photo of a woman and a young boy on Dominic's shoulders.

Dominic bends down to grab the photo for a bit. 

DOMINIC
Do you have kids?

(action)
Dominic hands Dallas the photo. 

DALLAS
No. 

DOMINIC
(looking at the photo)
I had a family once. 

(action)
Dallas falls mute. 

DOMINIC
No kid now, no wife now. Just my brother. And our uncle Mike.
(beat)
Not blood, maybe. But that's the house. My lot in life. I'll take it.

(action)
Dominic reaches out for a handshake. 

DOMINIC
It has been nice hanging out with you Dallas - thanks for coming and helping today. 

DALLAS
Oh you're welcome. 

(action)
Dominic scans the room and finds his gaze upon the corner. MR. MIKE (mid 70s) begins to wake up from his  rocking chair, staring fixedly at the floor. Mr. Mike scans the room and then outside and then takes a deep breath before he begins to violintely scratch the wood of the table using a jagged nail. 

DOMINIC 
Mr. Mike! Mr. Mike, what are you doing?! 

(action)
Mr. Mike suddenly looks up, his eyes milky but sharp. He points a finger at the window.

MR. MIKE
It's starting again.

(action)
Dominic opens the door. Asher enters and kneels beside Mr. Mike.

(action)
Mr. Mike points to the window. 

(action)
Asher's eyes widen when he sees what Mr. Mike has scratched into the table. He already has his sticky note out; he copies the marks, taps the page twice, then folds the note and puts it in his pocket. 

(action)
Dominic moves toward the kitchen and stumbles upon Dallas's heavy audio recorder.

DOMINIC 
What is this? A boat anchor?

DALLAS
It's a recorder. 

DOMINIC
A recorder? You taping this conversation? I'm kidding, you could, la la la la laaaa. Dallas do you want to have some dinner with us? 

DALLAS
Um, no - I should be going back. Thank you though.  

DOMINIC
You sure? 

DALLAS
Yeah, I got some food back at home, so, I'll be okay.  

(action)
Mr. Mike in the distance begins to get agitated. He rocks slowly in his rocking chair and taps his fingers. 

MR. MIKE
The song's gone wrong.

(action)
Asher is still kneeling beside Mr. Mike, listening intently. Dominic looks over from the kitchen.

DOMINIC
(from the Kitchen)
Mr. Mike, calm down my friend - dinner will be ready soon. 

DOMINIC
Asher, I showed Dallas your little basement dwelling.

(action)
Asher looks over to Dallas for a beat, then back at Mr. Mike.

(action)
Dallas's eyes drift up to the strings of tiny colored beads hung across the ceiling — a hanging model of something, dozens of nodes on threads. Dominic follows his gaze.

DOMINIC
Asher's map. Every bead's a spot out in the grass he's tagged. He keeps the whole field up there — none of us can.

(action)
Asher, meanwhile, ignores the conversation, eyes fixed on the distant bushes at the edge of the property. The leaves flutter, though there is no wind. His finger traces a ripple on his hand.

(action)
Dominic begins to light the gas to start boiling the brussels but the lighter doesnt work. 
He throws up his hands and steps outside to the patio to get it going.

(action)
Dallas stands near the table, holding the frame with the photo of Dominic's family. He sets it down gently. Mr. Mike rocks in his chair. He has stopped muttering. His eyes are on Dallas now. Focused. Different.

MR. MIKE
Come here.

(action)
Dallas glances toward the patio. Dominic is out of earshot. He walks over.

MR. MIKE
Come closer I can't hear you.

(action)
Dallas crouches beside the rocking chair. Mr. Mike reaches out and takes Dallas's wrist. His grip is stronger than it should be.

MR. MIKE
You listen to me.

(action)
Dallas doesn't move.

MR. MIKE
Javerias told me this when I was seven. My father's age. I'm telling you now. You're going to need it.

(action)
A beat. Mr. Mike's eyes are clear. No fog. No wandering.

MR. MIKE
If you find one of the bright ones. Hurt. Bleeding. Anything.

(action)
He tightens his grip on Dallas's wrist.

MR. MIKE
You leave it.

DALLAS
(quiet)
What?

MR. MIKE
You don't touch it. You don't take it. You don't try to help. You leave it.

(action)
Mr. Mike looks at him for a long moment. His lip trembles.

MR. MIKE
If you touch the hurt one, you make the dark one. And then you become the dark one.

(action)
Dallas stares at him.

MR. MIKE
Every time. Every single time.

(action)
He lets go of Dallas's wrist.

MR. MIKE
I touched one. When I was a boy. I didn't know. Javerias came the next week. Told me what I did.

(action)
A pause. The rocker creaks once.

MR. MIKE
The dark one is still out there.

(action)
Dallas stares out the window and on the floor.

From outside:
DOMINIC (O.S.)
Dallas — you want water?

(action)
The spell breaks. Mr. Mike blinks. His eyes cloud over again. He looks at Dallas like he's not sure who he is.

MR. MIKE
(confused, gentle)
Who are you?

(action)
Dallas stands slowly. His hand goes to his own wrist — rubbing where Mr. Mike gripped it.

DALLAS
(calling back)
No, I'm okay.

(action)
Mr. Mike starts humming.

(action)
Dallas looks at his wrist one more time, then at the rocking chair. Then at the window, toward the tall grass.

EXT. DOMINIC'S PATIO (s07.3)

DOMINIC
Thank you again Dallas.

(action)
Dallas begins to walk across the street to his house.
