## Modified Production Approach

### **Simplifications for Micro-Budget**

1. **Fewer Locations**
   - Skip some street coverage (consolidate to 2-3 key streets)
   - Use same room for multiple location purposes (office = mayor's office = detective room)
   - Skip elaborate ballroom if no access (shoot Mayor's office scenes only)

2. **Minimal Lighting Rigs**
   - Boatyard: Use existing darkness + small LED panels (battery-powered, $0 if borrowed)
   - Office: Practical overhead lights only
   - Bar: Existing neon + practicals
   - Street: Natural light + practical streetlights

3. **No Crowd Scenes or Massive Extras**
   - Ballroom: 20-30 friends instead of 500
   - Bar: 5-10 friends instead of 20
   - Sailors pub: 8-12 friends instead of 40

4. **Single Camera Operation**
   - Shoot A camera only (Director's camera)
   - No B camera for coverage variety
   - More takes per scene, strategic shot list

5. **Amateur Stunt Work**
   - Boatyard fight: Low-intensity choreography, friends as henchmen
   - No professional stunt coordinator (director choreographs, friends understand safety)
   - Emphasis on camera angles + editing (not physical danger)

6. **DIY Sound**
   - Record dialogue on-set with smartphone lav mic
   - Voiceover: Record Dustin actor's monologue in friend's quiet bedroom
   - Post-production sound: Director edits audio, minimal sound design

7. **No Post-Production Color Grading**
   - Shoot color-balanced in-camera (set white balance, exposure)
   - Minimal color correction in editing (DaVinci Resolve free version or equivalent)
   - Noir aesthetic from practical lighting + costume + blocking (not heavy grading)

## Recommendations for Success

1. **Lock Cast Early** — All friends must commit to full 14 days (or clear schedule for their scenes). No last-minute cancellations.

2. **Scout Locations Now** — Contact all location owners ASAP. Confirm dates + access times. Get written confirmation.

3. **Test Equipment** — Borrow camera/audio/lights NOW. Test before shoot. Ensure everything works.

4. **Detailed Shot List** — Director creates detailed shot list per day (max 15-20 shots/day). Keeps schedule tight.

5. **Rehearse Fight Choreography** — Day 8 boatyard fight requires rehearsal. Practice 2-3 times before shoot.

6. **Boatyard Safety** — Designate safety friend. Brief everyone on water hazard. Rope + rescue plan.

7. **Backup Plans** — Bad weather? Have interior backup scenes. Friend cancels? Have contingency casting.

8. **Manage Energy** — 14 days is exhausting. Build in lighter days. Night shoots are hardest (days 2, 8-9).

9. **Protect Audio** — Production audio is everything. Invest time in good lav mic setup. Clean takes = less post-work.

10. **Trust the Script** — If dialogue + voiceover are strong, visual DIY quality doesn't matter. Story carries it.
