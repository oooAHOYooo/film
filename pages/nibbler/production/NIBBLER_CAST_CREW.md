# CAST

## Principal Cast

### **DUSTIN MEGLIO / "The Nibbler"**
- **Role:** Detective protagonist, voiceover narrator
- **Episode Appearances:** All 3 episodes (lead throughout)
- **Key Scenes:** E1_01-09, E2_01-07, E3_01-09 (every major scene)
- **Character Description:** 
  - Age: 42-48
  - A worn, quiet detective known on the streets as "The Nibbler"
  - Methodical investigator, carries suppressed history with Gael
  - Voiceover narration in noir style (dry, occasionally humorous)
  - Physical presence: gaunt, sharp eyes, moves with deliberation
  - Costume: Detective outfit (blazer, worn shirts, practical shoes)

- **Actor Requirements:**
  - Strong voiceover capability (carries entire series)
  - Able to convey suppressed emotion subtly (the past with Gael)
  - Physicality for hand-to-hand fight choreography
  - Comfortable with extended monologues and voiceover recording

- **Screen Time:** ~45 minutes across 3 episodes
- **Availability:** All 14 shooting days (present in 95% of scenes)

# CREW

## Executive Leadership

### **Producer**
- **Responsibilities:** Overall project management, financing, distribution deals, production oversight
- **Reports To:** Financial backers
- **Budget Authority:** Sign-off on major line items
- **Availability:** Strategic presence, on-set key decision moments

### **Executive Producer(s)** (typically 1-2)
- **Responsibilities:** Financing facilitation, strategic partnerships, festival submissions
- **Reports To:** Producer
- **Availability:** Consultation role, not on-set day-to-day

### **Line Producer**
- **Responsibilities:** Day-to-day budget management, crew hiring, vendor negotiation
- **Reports To:** Producer
- **Budget Authority:** Approve daily expenses, manage contingency reserves
- **Availability:** Full-time, on-set (ensures budget adherence)

- **Responsibilities:**

- **Reports To:** Producer (creatively autonomous within budget)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Assemble footage from principal photography
  - Rough cut assembly
  - Fine cut refinement (working with Director)
  - Pacing, transitions, visual storytelling

- **Reports To:** Director
- **Availability:** Post-production (weeks 1-8)
- **Schedule:** 12-16 week post-production timeline
- **Experience Requirement:** Feature-level editor, experience with episodic/serialized content

---

### **Color Grader**
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Color correction (camera white balance, exposure consistency)
  - Color grading (noir aesthetic, episode-specific palettes)
  - LUT development (on-set reference color)

- **Reports To:** Director / DP
- **Availability:** Post-production (weeks 6-10)
- **Experience Requirement:** Feature-level color grading, noir/stylized work

- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Episode 1: Cold fluorescent office reality
  - Episode 2: Warm amber ballroom, screen glare realism
  - Episode 3: Cold-warm shift (flashback cold → boatyard industrial → epilogue warmth)
  - Shipyard Flashback: Desaturated blue-cyan (E1_04)

---

### **Sound Designer**
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Create sonic landscape (ambience, effects, score coordination)
  - Dialogue mixing prep
  - Spatial audio for key locations (boatyard water, ballroom reverb, office hum)

- **Reports To:** Director / Post-Production Supervisor
- **Availability:** Post-production (weeks 8-14)
- **Experience Requirement:** Feature-level sound design, noir audio aesthetics

---

### **Dialogue Editor / Sound Mixer (Post)**
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Clean up production dialogue
  - ADR (Additional Dialogue Recording) if needed
  - Voiceover integration (Dustin's monologue)
  - Dialogue mixing to final mix

- **Reports To:** Sound Designer
- **Availability:** Post-production (weeks 6-12)
- **Critical for Nibbler:** Voiceover quality is essential to noir format

---

### **Composer / Music Supervisor**
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Original score composition (noir themes)
  - Music licensing (if using existing noir/jazz standards)
  - Temp tracks for editing reference
  - Final score delivery

- **Reports To:** Director
- **Availability:** Post-production consultation (weeks 1-4), score composition (weeks 8-14)
- **Experience Requirement:** Feature-level composition, noir/period scoring experience

---

### **Visual Effects Supervisor** (if needed)
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Screen reveals (E2_04 ballroom screens showing scandal footage)
  - Magnet effect (gun pull, E3_04)
  - Crowd enhancement if ballroom crowd underside (modern standard)
  - Any minor CG/compositing

- **Reports To:** Director / Post-Production Supervisor
- **Availability:** Post-production consultation (weeks 1-2), VFX execution (weeks 6-12)
- **Scope:** Minimal — this is primarily practical production effects

  - Oversee entire post-production pipeline
  - Scheduling and vendor coordination

  - Delivery specifications (streaming platforms, theatrical, etc.)

## Union & Contracts

### **SAG-AFTRA Compliance**
- **Principal Cast:** Union agreements (SAG-AFTRA likely for lead roles)
- **Supporting Cast:** Mix of union/non-union based on role size
- **Extras:** Non-union typical for background/crowd (may negotiate union per ballroom day)

### **Crew Unions**
- **IATSE (International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees):** Camera, Grip, Electric, Sound departments
- **Typical Features:** Union crew standard for feature productions ($2.5M+ budget)
- **Negotiation:** Production may negotiate IATSE rates for low-budget indie feature (Day rates vs. hourly)
