## Location 5: Grand Ballroom / City Hall

### **Physical Description**
- **Type:** Interior ballroom + exterior facade
- **Dimensions:** Ballroom ~3,000-5,000 sq ft (high ceilings 20+ feet)
- **Scenes:**
  - E2_01 (City Hall exterior approach)
  - E2_03 (Grand Ballroom — Mayor's speech, crowd, Gael entrance)
  - E2_04 (Screens go live — chaos, crowd reaction)
  - E2_05 (Dustin sees Gael's face)
  - E2_07 (Backstage with Mayor — quick setup)
- **Shoot Days:** 4, 10 (MAJOR production days)
- **Estimated Runtime:** ~18 minutes

### **Location Requirements**
- **Ballroom Interior:**
  - Clear floor space: 2,000+ sq ft
  - Ceiling height: 20+ feet (allows overhead rig)
  - Stage/Podium: Existing or buildable (6'x8' minimum)
  - Crowd Capacity: 300-500 extras positioning
  - Chandelier/Overhead Fixtures: Existing architectural interest
  - Mirrors/Reflective Surfaces: For lighting versatility

- **Technical Infrastructure:**
  - Large projection screens (2) for ballroom screens (12'+ wide)
  - Video input capability (Gael screens reveal Mayor scandal footage)
  - Audio system: Existing PA system
  - Power: Multiple 100A circuits available (heavy lighting load)

- **Backstage Area:**
  - Separate room or alcove (Mayor private moment)
  - Door to main ballroom
  - Lighting control area (accessible)

- **Exterior:**
  - Building facade for approach shots
  - Parking area: 50+ spaces (crew + extras)
  - Entrance doors: Operable, grand (City Hall aesthetic)

### **Cinematography Specs**
- **WIDE SHOT EMPHASIS** — This is the series centerpiece
- **Overhead Chandelier:** High-mounted gimbal looking downward into crowd (4K)
- **Stage-to-Audience:** 35-50mm wide from stage level capturing crowd depth
- **Crowd Parting:** Steadicam/gimbal through crowd maintaining ballroom edges
- **Screen Reflections:** Wide shots capturing screens + reflected crowd faces simultaneously
- **Sight-Line Wides:** Ballroom width allows sight-line composition (Dustin sees Gael across room)
- **Crowd Chaos:** Wide medium-shots maintain choreography clarity (no breakdown of arena feel)

### **Practical Needs**
- **Electrical:** Two 200A service panels minimum (heavy lighting rig)
  - 12K HMI
  - 6K HMI
  - Arri SkyPanel array (color/intensity control)
  - LED fresnels (practical ballroom lighting)
  
- **Rigging:** Full crane/lift access to ceiling
  - Gimbal/camera rig overhead mount
  - Chandelier practical wiring modifications
  - Cable runs from control area

- **Staging:** Portable stage sections (if existing podium insufficient)

- **Parking:** 50+ crew + extras spaces (must negotiate with City Hall)

- **Permits:** Major filming permit, city liaison required

- **Crowd Extras:** Casting 300-500 crowd (professional extras or verified locals)
  - Wardrobe: Formal attire (suits, dresses for gala)
  - Positioning: Pre-shoot blocking / choreography
  - Food Service: Minimal catering on set (extras don't eat full crew meals)

- **Safety:** 
  - Crowd control barriers
  - Clear egress routes (fire code)
  - Medical standby (large crowd)
  - Safety briefing for stunt/action moments

- **Restrooms:** Adequate facilities for large crew + crowd

- **Weather:** Indoor location (no weather contingency needed)

### **Scout Notes**
- **Ideal:** Actual City Hall ballroom or historic event venue with grand ballroom
- **Preferred:** Art Deco or Beaux-Arts architecture (period appropriate)
- **Must-Have:** High ceilings, impressive scale, chandeliers, stage/podium space
- **Avoid:** Modern hotel ballrooms (wrong aesthetic), low ceilings, stark minimalism
- **Nice-to-Have:** Mirrors, marble, ornate railings (photogenic architecture)

### **Pre-Production Coordination**
- **Location Lock:** 4 weeks before shoot (permits, crowd casting begins)
- **Tech Scout:** 2 weeks before (lighting rigging plan, camera positions, screen placement)
- **Final Walk:** 1 week before (last-minute coordination with venue)

### **Access & Hours**
- **Shoot Days:** Full location takeover, 6am-10pm (2 full days)
- **Setup:** 5-6 hours prior (lighting rig, stage, screens, crowd positioning)
- **Breakdown:** 4-5 hours post-shoot (restore chandeliers, remove rigging)
- **Location Closure:** 48 hours (setup + shoot + breakdown)

### **Budget Estimate**
- **Location Fee:** $10,000-20,000 (major institutional venue)
- **Permits & City Coordination:** $2,000-5,000

- **Catering (Crew + Minimized Extras):** $4,000-6,000
- **Security & Crowd Control:** $1,500-2,500
- **Insurance Add-On (Crowd):** $500-1,000

## Location 6: Boatyard / Docks

### **Physical Description**
- **Type:** Exterior maritime industrial environment
- **Dimensions:** Large waterfront area, 200+ meters long
- **Scenes:**
  - E1_09 (Boatyard Surveillance setup — night, Ghost monitoring signals)
  - E3_04 (Boatyard Trap — henchmen emerge, magnet trigger)
  - E3_05 (Gael calls Dustin by first name)
  - E3_06 (The Fight — team hand-to-hand combat)
  - E3_07 (Hostages — chase sequence, rescue)
  - E3_08 (Aftermath — quiet moment, water reflection)
- **Shoot Days:** 2, 8, 9 (THREE full days — critical location)
- **Estimated Runtime:** ~20 minutes

### **Location Requirements**
- **Waterfront Access:**
  - Docked boats/shipping vessels (visual texture)
  - Clear open ground: 300+ ft (allows wide arena setup)
  - Water edge: Deep enough to reflect light (not muddy beach)
  - Dock structures: Weathered wood/metal (authentic industrial)

- **Shipping/Industrial Elements:**
  - Containers (stacked or movable for fight choreography)
  - Dock equipment: Cranes, pulleys, ropes (set dressing)
  - Warehouse building or storage (backstage entry for henchmen)
  - Surveillance camera positions (functional or practical only)

- **Lighting Infrastructure:**
  - Minimal existing ambient light (location is dark at night)
  - Electrical access: 200A minimum (HMI rigs for night shoots)
  - Reflective surfaces: Water + concrete for light play
  - Overhead rigging: Possible on warehouse structure

- **Access & Safety:**
  - Gate/access control (secure set)
  - Non-public location preferred (minimizes bystander interference)
  - Emergency vehicle access (ambulance, fire truck clearance)
  - Water safety: Life jackets available (proximity to deep water)

### **Cinematography Specs**
- **AERIAL/DRONE WIDE SHOTS:** Entire boatyard perimeter establishing (4K for editorial)
- **Ground-Level Wides:** 50-100mm long-lens compression, Dustin small in dock landscape
- **Water Reflections:** Tungsten + HMI lighting on water surface (practical beauty)
- **Arena Wide:** Fight sequence maintains constant wide-medium shots (never tighter than medium)
- **Silhouette Work:** Night-time backlit figures against water/industrial horizon
- **Silent Wides:** Empty boatyard post-action (post-production sound design space)
- **Chase Perspective:** Wide tracking of hostage rescue (maintaining arena scale)

### **Stunt/Action Coordination**
- **Stunt Coordinator:** On-site for all 3 days
- **Fight Choreography:** Team vs. henchmen in open ground (no obstacles, clear movement)
- **Magnet Prop FX:** Practical rigged guns (magnetic "pull" effect in camera/post)
- **Fall/Collision Safety:** Padding, mats concealed in frame, medical on standby
- **Water Safety:** Rescue swimmers positioned if any water-adjacent stunts

### **Practical Needs**
- **Electrical:** Two 200A service panels (HMI 12K + 6K heavy night-time load)
  - Generator truck possible (loud, schedule hum-free takes)
  - Cable runs across boatyard (trip hazard management)

- **Parking:** 15-20 crew vehicles + equipment trucks (40+ spaces ideal)

- **Permits:** 
  - Maritime/port authority filming permit
  - Police coordination (active dock operations)
  - Environmental compliance (if near protected waters)

- **Crew Facilities:**
  - Craft services in trailers (open-air location, weather)
  - Restroom facilities (portable units or nearby building)
  - Heated tent/staging area (night shoots, cold water proximity)

- **Weather Contingency:**
  - Waterfront wind/weather unpredictable
  - Rain pushes day schedule (HMI rigs incompatible with rain)
  - Backup location: Move to interior ballroom if severe weather

- **Crowd/Extras:**
  - Henchmen: 15-20 costume/armed appearance
  - Hostages: 8-10 civilians for rescue scene
  - All require stunt training or safety briefing

- **Safety Briefings:**
  - Water proximity briefing (crew + extras)
  - Stunt/fight choreography walkthrough
  - Night shooting protocols (reflective vests, careful placement)
  - Equipment rigging safety (overhead cables)

- **Must-Have:** Open ground for arena setup, water for reflections, dock structures

- **Nice-to-Have:** Period boatyard equipment, weathered wood, visible water activity
