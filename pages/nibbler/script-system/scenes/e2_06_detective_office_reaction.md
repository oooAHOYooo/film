INT. DETECTIVE OFFICE - NIGHT

The monitor bank replays the ballroom footage on a loop: the screens going live, the Mayor stammering, the man in the suit beside the podium.

Dustin, the Ghost, and Lucy — Dustin and Lucy still half in catering whites. The Ghost freezes the frame on the man in the suit.

GHOST

No mask. No costume. He showed four hundred people his face.

LUCY

Because his face isn't in anything. I'd bet my tips on it. No record, no file, no name in any system you can reach with a badge.

She leans toward the screen, reading the frozen frame like a difficult customer.

LUCY

Look at the room. He owned it before he walked on stage. The footage, the timing, the screens — weeks of work. You don't do weeks of work to steal from a coat check.

(turning to them)

This was never about the robberies. The robberies were rehearsal. He wants to humiliate the Mayor — take him apart in public, in front of everyone who ever shook his hand.

GHOST

"The Weevil sends his regards."

LUCY

Right. So he's not the Weevil. He's the delivery.

The Ghost looks at Dustin — who has been silent this whole time, staring at the frozen frame.

GHOST

You're quiet.

DUSTIN

I'm thinking.

GHOST

You're standing.

Dustin is, in fact, already standing, coat half on.

DUSTIN

The Mayor's about to be the most radioactive man in Connecticut. Every friend he has is deleting his number right now. Which makes tonight the one night he'll talk to people like us.

LUCY

People like us being?

DUSTIN

The only ones still in the building.

FADE OUT.
