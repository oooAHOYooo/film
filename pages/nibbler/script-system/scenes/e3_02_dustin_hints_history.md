INT. CITY HALL - MAYOR'S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS

The Mayor pours two glasses of the scotch after all. Slides one across. Dustin doesn't touch it.

MAYOR ANDERSON

You understand what I'm asking you to carry. If it gets out that the Weevil is an Anderson—

DUSTIN

It won't get out from me.

MAYOR ANDERSON

Why do I believe you?

DUSTIN

Because I'm the only man in this city with less to gain from talking than you.

The Mayor almost smiles. He drinks. Dustin looks past him, at the wall of photographs — and something in his face changes. The detective going somewhere else.

DUSTIN

(almost to himself)

I knew someone connected to Mark. A long time ago. Before any of this.

The Mayor lowers his glass.

MAYOR ANDERSON

Connected how?

Dustin doesn't answer.

MAYOR ANDERSON

Dustin. Connected how?

DUSTIN

(standing)

It was a long time ago.

He buttons his coat. The subject is closed — visibly, deliberately, with the finality of a man locking a door from the inside.

The Mayor searches his face the way he did backstage. This time he finds the locked door, and he is politician enough to know one when he sees it.

MAYOR ANDERSON

We all have our Maines, Detective.

DUSTIN

(V.O.)

He was right. And mine didn't have a facility or a family fortune. Mine had a name I hadn't said out loud in years — and I wasn't going to start in the Mayor's office.

FADE OUT.
