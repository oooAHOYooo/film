INT. RUDIE'S BAR - LATER

The door opens. Nobody announces anything, and yet the room shifts.

MAX — known on the street as THE GHOST — stands in the doorway. 40s. Long coat. He has the stillness of a man who has already checked the exits. The pool game pauses. A woman at the bar sits up a little straighter.

The Ghost crosses to Dustin and sits. He does not order. Lance brings him a beer anyway.

DUSTIN

(V.O.)

Max. The Ghost. My trusted partner — elusive, mysterious, loyal past the point of reason. He lost everything once for telling the truth. I've never been entirely sure why he chose this life after what it cost him. I've never asked. That's the arrangement.

They drink in silence for a moment. It is a comfortable silence, or at least a well-worn one.

GHOST

The pawn shop on Whalley.

DUSTIN

Eleven now.

GHOST

Figurine?

DUSTIN

Figurine.

The Ghost nods slowly. Drinks.

GHOST

Tell me about the shipyard.

Dustin's hand stops halfway to his glass.

DUSTIN

Who told you about the shipyard?

GHOST

You did. Just now.

Dustin exhales. Puts the glass down.

DUSTIN

He was sitting at a desk, Max. A desk. In the middle of the yard, like it was a Tuesday at the bank. Broken glasses all over the ground — his little trail, his little joke. I had the drop on him. Gun out, cuffs out.

GHOST

And?

DUSTIN

Smoke. A magician's exit. Gone.

GHOST

You saw his face.

A pause. Dustin looks at the bottles behind the bar.

DUSTIN

I saw his eyes. Calm. Not the calm of a man who doesn't care. The calm of a man who's already planned the next four moves.

Dustin opens his mouth to say something more — something specific — and closes it again.

GHOST

(watching him)

What.

DUSTIN

Nothing. Finish your beer.

FADE OUT.
