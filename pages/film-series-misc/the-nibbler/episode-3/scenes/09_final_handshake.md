INT. CITY HALL - MAYOR'S OFFICE - DAY

Daylight, for once. The team assembled — the Ghost by the window, Lucy in the good chair, Dustin standing. On the Mayor's desk, in an evidence bag: one weevil figurine.

MAYOR ANDERSON

The press will spend a month on the gala. The department will spend a year on the boatyard. Mark is counting on both. Which means the four of us are the only people in New Haven working on what actually matters.

He comes around the desk and extends his hand to Dustin.

MAYOR ANDERSON

Together, we'll be unstoppable.

Dustin takes it. The handshake is firm, formal, a little ridiculous, and entirely sincere — which is, by now, the house style.

Lucy shakes next. Then the Ghost, who shakes hands like he's returning something.

LUCY

So what do we call it? A team needs a name.

GHOST

No it doesn't.

LUCY

It absolutely does.

They file out, arguing quietly. Dustin lingers a moment — one last look at the figurine on the desk — then follows.

EXT. CITY HALL - DAY

Dustin comes down the steps alone, onto the street. New Haven going about its business: buses, students, a man selling flowers, the ordinary city that has no idea.

He puts in a wad of chewing tobacco. Holds it. Spits it out.

DUSTIN

(V.O.)

The city still has shadows. Mark Anderson is still out there, building his new city inside the old one. And Gael is still out there too.

He walks. The camera holds on him, small against the block.

DUSTIN

(V.O.)

He called me by my name. That means he's been watching. That means whatever this is, it didn't start at the shipyard. It started long before that — and I'm the only one on the team who knows how long.

(beat)

I don't know what he wants from me yet. But I know this. The Nibbler has a crew now. And whatever Gael is building for the Weevil — we're going to find it before he finishes.

He stops at the corner. For a moment, in a shop window's reflection, he checks the street behind him.

Nothing. Just the city.

He keeps walking.

FADE TO BLACK.
