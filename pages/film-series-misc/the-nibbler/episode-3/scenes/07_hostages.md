EXT. BOATYARD - DOCKS - CONTINUOUS

The henchmen haul the hostages down the long dock toward a waiting boat, the whole procession absurd and terrible in the moonlight — giant weevils dragging hooded prisoners past sleeping sailboats.

LUCY

We can't let them get away with innocent lives!

DUSTIN

We won't. Hostages first. The Weevil can wait.

They run. The Ghost cuts left, vanishing between boat cradles — gone, the way he goes.

ON THE DOCK: the lead henchman hears footsteps, turns—

—and the Ghost is already in front of him, having arrived from nowhere and, apparently, earlier. The henchman freezes mid-step, bat raised.

GHOST

Put it down. You look ridiculous.

The henchman looks down at his own segmented costume. Some deep truth lands on him. He puts the bat down.

The others don't. Dustin and Lucy hit the dock at a run — Dustin low-tackles one henchman off the edge into the water. Lucy hooks a boat gaff into another's costume padding and swings him, with great force and impressive leverage, into a piling.

The Mayor, wheezing far behind:

MAYOR ANDERSON

(shouting)

I am ORDERING you to surrender! I'm still technically the Mayor!

Remarkably, one henchman hesitates — long enough for the Ghost to relieve him of consciousness.

Dustin cuts the hostages free — a DOCKWORKER, a NIGHT WATCHMAN, a TEENAGER who was fishing in the wrong place. The remaining henchmen abandon the boat and scatter into the dark, shedding costume parts as they run — a bulbous insect head bobbing away between the hulls, then nothing.

Lucy pulls the hood off the last hostage. Checks him with bartender efficiency.

LUCY

You're okay. Breathe. You're okay.

Dustin stands at the end of the dock, looking out at the black water. No boat. No suit. No Gael.

FADE OUT.
