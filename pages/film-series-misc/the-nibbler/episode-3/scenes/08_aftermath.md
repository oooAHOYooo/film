EXT. BOATYARD - LATER

Quiet again. Ambulance lights at the gate, distant. The hostages wrapped in blankets. Two henchmen zip-tied to the crane column, next to the guns still magnetized to it — someone will have to figure that out.

The team stands together in the gravel, catching their breath. The Mayor looks out at the dark water.

MAYOR ANDERSON

We may not have captured him tonight. But we saved innocent lives.

(beat)

Our fight continues.

LUCY

(exhausted)

You really do talk like that.

MAYOR ANDERSON

Thirty years of ribbon-cuttings. It gets in the blood.

Lucy walks the desk in the center of the yard. The four figurines still sit in their neat row. She picks one up, turns it in the light.

LUCY

Four of them. He knew exactly who was coming.

She sets it back down — then notices Dustin isn't listening. He is looking at the gap between the containers where Gael vanished.

The Ghost appears at Dustin's shoulder, quiet as ever.

GHOST

He called you by your first name.

Dustin doesn't answer.

GHOST

In front of everyone. Like it was normal.

Dustin doesn't answer.

The Ghost studies his partner's profile a moment longer. Then he nods — not agreement, just a receipt. The question has been filed. It will be asked again.

GHOST

Okay.

He walks off toward the ambulances. Dustin stays, looking at the gap in the containers, alone in the lamplight with a desk full of figurines.

FADE OUT.
