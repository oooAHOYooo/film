INT. CITY HALL - BACKSTAGE - NIGHT

The party is a crime scene now. Overturned chairs through the doorway, abandoned champagne, someone's single shoe. Backstage, among the road cases, MAYOR ANDERSON sits alone on a folding chair — tie undone, face gray. A man watching thirty years of handshakes catch fire.

Dustin, the Ghost, and Lucy approach. The Mayor's security detail moves to block; the Mayor waves them off with a tired hand.

MAYOR ANDERSON

Detectives. Come to arrest me? You'd have to take a number.

DUSTIN

We're not here for that, Mayor.

LUCY

You were set up. The footage was cut to bleed — real pieces arranged into the worst possible shape. Whoever built that reel spent weeks on it. This wasn't news. It was a production.

MAYOR ANDERSON

(a hollow laugh)

A production. Yes. He always did like a production.

The word "he" sits there. Dustin catches it. Files it.

DUSTIN

The man on the stage. You saw him?

MAYOR ANDERSON

I saw him.

DUSTIN

Do you know him?

A pause that is one beat too long.

MAYOR ANDERSON

No.

The Mayor looks up at Dustin then — really looks, searching his face for something. Whatever he finds, it decides him.

MAYOR ANDERSON

I've always trusted your team, Detective. Even when the department didn't. Especially then. And now I need you more than ever.

DUSTIN

We'll find who did this.

MAYOR ANDERSON

I know you will. That's rather what I'm afraid of.

He stands, straightens his ruined tie out of pure muscle memory.

MAYOR ANDERSON

Dustin. There's one more thing. Not here.

(beat)

Come to my office.

He walks off between the road cases, security falling in around him. The team watches him go.

LUCY

What's in his office?

DUSTIN

(V.O.)

I didn't know yet. But I knew the sound of a man about to stop lying. It's quieter than you'd think.

FADE OUT.

END OF EPISODE 2.
