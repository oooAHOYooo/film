INT. CITY HALL - GRAND BALLROOM - CONTINUOUS

Chaos in full flower. Guests surge toward the exits. Security wades against the current. Lucy shepherds a cluster of panicking caterers toward the kitchen.

Dustin fights through the crowd toward the stage, tray long gone, catering jacket half off—

—and stops.

Across the ballroom, at the mouth of a service corridor, moving against the crowd with no more urgency than a man leaving a dull party: the man in the suit.

For one clean second the crowd opens between them. Full light. Full face.

GAEL.

Dustin freezes. Completely. A rock in a river of fleeing tuxedos.

DUSTIN

(V.O.)

(very quiet)

There he was.

Gael does not stop. But his eyes find Dustin's across the whole width of the room — the same half-second as the shipyard, going both directions.

And then, barely, almost kindly: Gael inclines his head. A greeting. Unmistakable. Meant for one person.

He turns into the corridor and is gone.

DUSTIN

(V.O.)

And I knew him. I had always known him.

LUCY

(arriving, breathless)

Dustin — the stage — did you see him? Did you get a look at his face?

Dustin doesn't answer. He is still looking at the empty corridor.

LUCY

Dustin.

DUSTIN

(coming back)

Yeah. I got a look.

FADE OUT.
