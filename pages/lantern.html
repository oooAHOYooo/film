About This Project

Codename: Lantern

Setting: New Haven, Connecticut — Winter

Logline: A shy Taiwanese postdoc researcher in New Haven discovers his left hand harbors an uncontrollable heat-generating power rooted in forgotten mythology. When he accidentally injures someone, he spirals into isolation—until forced to use the power to save someone he cares about, exposing himself to a world that doesn't know what to make of him.

Key Themes

  • Coming of Age Through Loss of Control — He discovers power precisely when he thought he understood his life.
  • Isolation vs. Visibility — From complete solitude to unwanted attention. Neither state is comfortable.
  • Inherited Burden — The power connects to Taiwanese heritage he may not fully understand.
  • Gentleness in Conflict — A good-hearted, shy person wielding dangerous force.

Visual Language

Winter Aesthetic: Muted, cool-toned New Haven winter. Frost, grey skies, architectural textures. His heat power contrasts sharply—warmth, glow, fire against cold surroundings.

The Left Hand: Visually marked or glowing. Constantly hidden—gloves, pockets, sleeves. Tension builds around moments when it might be exposed.

Production Notes

  • Shot in New Haven; RED Komodo 6K available
  • Winter timing aligns with production availability
  • Dormitory/apartment interiors provide intimate, constrained spaces
  • New Haven's architecture and harbor offer visual richness
  • Minimal supernatural effects needed—focus on practical heat, light, performance
