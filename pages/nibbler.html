Episode Outlines

Episode 1

The Trail

Act 1 of the series · Setup & character

  • 01Crime scene. Dustin works a scene — magnifying glass, tweezers. Voiceover establishes who he is and what the Weevil case is. He pauses on the evidence bag. A figurine inside. Something crosses his face.
  • 02The trail. Outside the station — chewing tobacco, spits it immediately. Spots a pair of glasses on the sidewalk. Then another. Follows the trail. It ends at Rudie's Bar.
  • 03Rudie's. The regulars love him. He picks up the paper: "Mayor's Japan Scandal Resurfaces." Reads it. Voiceover circles a thought it doesn't finish.
  • 04Shipyard flashback. Dustin had Gael cornered. Drew his weapon. A half-second of recognition between them — then smoke, and Gael is gone. Dustin left holding cuffs, looking like a fool.
  • 05The Ghost walks in. Max arrives without announcement. The bar shifts. They drink. Dustin describes Gael's precision — the eyes. He almost says something more specific. He doesn't.
  • 06Back alley. Dustin tells the full story. Ghost listens. Did he see you? — Yes. And then he was gone. Ghost produces surveillance gear from his coat.
  • 07Detective office. Dustin presents his case. Jones and Miller laugh. The boss shuts them up but presses him. Dustin says he knows where the Weevil is moving. Long pause. Go find him. Don't embarrass us.
  • 08Outside the station. Self-doubt. Ghost: You're the reason I got into this. Dustin looks at the gear. Nods.
  • 09Boatyard, night. Surveillance rig. A signal breaks through the noise. Ghost unscrambles it. Two words on screen: MAYOR GRAND BALLROOM. I've got an idea.

Episode 2

The Party

Act 2 of the series · The plan meets the night

  • 01Find Lucy. They need inside the Mayor's gala. They need someone who knows catering, service entrances, staff rotations. They go to the seaside pub.
  • 02The pub. Shanties, noise, chaos. Lucy behind the bar — completely unbothered. They find a booth. Dustin explains. Lucy listens, then: Alright. What do I have to do? Dustin hesitates. Lucy: Trust me. Three hands clasp.
  • 03City Hall, night of the gala. Mayor Anderson takes the stage. Applause. Gael is already in the room — suit, no costume, just a man at a party. He moves to backstage, whispers to a technician.
  • 04The screens go live. Private footage of the Mayor: backroom deals, cash handoffs. The crowd goes silent. The Mayor stammers. Gael steps onto the stage — composed, almost serene. "It seems your Mayor's true nature has been exposed." Ballroom erupts.
  • 05Dustin sees his face. From somewhere in the crowd, Dustin sees Gael clearly for the first time since the shipyard. Voiceover, very quiet: There he was. And I knew him. I had always known him. He doesn't move for a beat too long.
  • 06Detective office. The team watches on screens. Lucy understands first: This was never about robbery. He wanted to destroy the Mayor. Dustin is already standing.
  • 07Backstage. The Mayor alone — pale, shaken. The team approaches. He was set up. He looks at Dustin. Something passes. I've always trusted your team. And now I need you. Then: There's one more thing. Come to my office.

Episode 3

Blood and Weevils

Act 3 of the series · The truth & the trap

  • 01Mayor's office. The Mayor tells Dustin the truth: the Weevil is his brother Mark. Sent to Maine, escaped, took the family fortune. He was the smarter one. I was just the one people liked. His voice breaks. He failed him.
  • 02Dustin absorbs it. Then, almost to himself: I knew someone who worked for him. A long time ago. He doesn't elaborate. The Mayor searches his face. Dustin is already standing.
  • 03The alliance. The four of them in the office. The Mayor proposes it formally: a team. A crew to take down the Weevil's network. Dustin is in before he finishes the sentence.
  • 04Boatyard, night. The team moves through the dark. Weevil henchmen step out — costumes, bats, a ring closing. Then Gael steps forward from the back. A suit. No costume. Calm.
  • 05The magnet. The team's guns fly from their hands. Gael looks at Dustin across the circle. "You should have stayed out of this, Dustin." Not Detective. Ghost looks at Dustin. Dustin doesn't look back.
  • 06The fight. Hand to hand. The team as a unit for the first time. Henchmen take damage. Gael slips the circle — unhurried — and is gone. His crew drags civilians toward the docks.
  • 07Hostages. Lucy: We can't let them get away with innocent lives. The team pivots, chases, frees them. Henchmen scatter. Gael is gone.
  • 08Aftermath. The Mayor, quietly: We may not have captured him. But we saved lives. Ghost to Dustin: He called you by your first name. Dustin says nothing.
  • 09The handshake. Mayor's office, daylight. The formal alliance. Together, we'll be unstoppable. Last shot: Dustin alone on the street. Voiceover closes the series.

About

In New Haven's shadow economy of secrets and favors, detective Dustin Meglio — known on the streets as The Nibbler — follows a trail of small, baffling clues straight into the biggest conspiracy the city has seen: a mayoral scandal, a phantom criminal known as the Weevil, and a Colombian operative named Gael who runs the Weevil's operation on the ground.

Gael is not the Weevil. He works for him. But he moves through New Haven like he owns it — precise, unhurried, cultured. He places the weevil figurines. He runs the henchmen. He calls Dustin by his first name at the boatyard, in front of everyone, like it's the most natural thing in the world. Like they have history.

They do. Series One plants it. Series Two excavates it.

Three episodes. 22 minutes each. Shot in New Haven on BMPCC4K.
